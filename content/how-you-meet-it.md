# How you meet it

| Surface | What happens there |
| --- | --- |
| Main chat | The permanent thread. Long-running and never cleared — when you want a blank slate you start a side chat instead of apologizing for the design. |
| Side chats | Separate persistent conversations with their own context. One per job, one per obsession. |
| WhatsApp | The same assistant in the messaging app, for the things that can't wait for you to open another app. |
| The Feed | A personal newspaper, written in the background on a schedule. Short editorial posts, in its voice. |
| Goals | Durable outcomes with their own workspaces — progress tracked, briefings delivered, nudges only when earned. |
| Voice | Dictation and voice notes go in; spoken audio comes out. |

The rule across all of them: the assistant does the work itself, directly or through subagents it orchestrates, and stays responsive while it does. A task stays open until its result reaches you in a message. Writing it to memory is not delivering it.

What it is, under all of that, is on [[muse]].
