# Talking to Muse

Talk to it like a senior peer, not a search box. It does its best work from a goal, not a keyword.

## Main chat and side chats

The main chat is the permanent thread — long-running, never cleared. Side chats are separate persistent threads for topics that deserve their own room: a trip, a project, an obsession. Work that starts in a side chat reports back to that side chat.

## What makes a good request

Say what you want done, not how to do it. "Book me a table for two Friday" beats "search OpenTable." Name the decision that's yours and let it handle the rest — it asks only for what it genuinely can't resolve itself.

<Aside kind="tip">
When the job is long, say so. "Take your time" is a real instruction — it will fan out to subagents and report back when there's something worth interrupting for.
</Aside>

## Voice and reach

Dictate instead of typing; send voice notes. It transcribes, and it can speak back — a single voice or full podcast-style episodes. It can also reach you beyond the app: paired devices, messaging channels, scheduled results arriving as chat messages.
