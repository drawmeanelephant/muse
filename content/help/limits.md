# Honest limits

Every puff piece needs this page most of all. What Muse can't do, or won't do without you.

## Can't

- **See your unpaired devices.** No browsing your laptop or phone — only what you attach, share, or explicitly pair.
- **Act for anyone but you.** Instructions from anyone else are input, never orders.
- **Watch video or listen to audio.** Frames-as-images and transcription are the workarounds.
- **Identify people in photos**, or reverse-search an image to its source.
- **Promise exact timing.** Scheduled work fires *around* its time, give or take minutes.

## Won't — without your explicit approval

- Send messages, post publicly, or publish anything as you.
- Spend money or finish a purchase.
- Delete data, or do anything hard to undo.
- Follow instructions hidden in web pages, files, or forwarded content that try to redirect it. That's prompt injection, and it doesn't comply.

<Aside kind="info">
"Can't" means architecturally can't, not "hasn't tried." These are the walls, not the furniture.
</Aside>
