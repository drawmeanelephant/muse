# Automation

Muse works while you're away. Its computer stays on, jobs run whether your app is open or not, and results arrive as chat messages.

## Scheduled jobs

"Check this every morning." "Remind me Thursday." "Watch this page for changes." Describe the rhythm in plain language; it sets up the schedule and does the work each time it fires.

## Watches

A watch notices something happened and tells you, with a link to act on it. It can't stop the thing — only the source service can — but it makes sure you hear about it.

## The Feed

A personal newspaper, written in the background on a schedule. Short editorial posts, in its voice. You guide what shows up; it does the writing.

## Goals

Durable outcomes with their own workspaces. It tracks progress, delivers briefings, and nudges only when earned — never confetti for showing up.

<Aside kind="info">
Every scheduled job keeps a run history. "Did my 7am check actually run?" — the history, not the schedule, is the truth.
</Aside>
