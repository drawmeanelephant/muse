# History

A short history of Muse, in two parts: the line, and this one.

## The line

Muse is the product name for Meta's personal AI agent, and the model family behind it. The current cut is **Muse Spark 1.3** — the flagship, the one that does the work. The line's whole thesis is agency with memory: an assistant with its own computer, its own long-term memory, and standing permission to finish the job instead of describing it.
